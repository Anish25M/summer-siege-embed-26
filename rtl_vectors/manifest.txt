Delta-sigma ADC RTL test-vector manifest
All .mem files contain one sample per line.
Raw MASH stages use 0/1 logic mapping: bipolar -1 -> 0, bipolar +1 -> 1.
Hexadecimal files use fixed-width signed two's-complement values.

Files:
mash_stage1_bits.mem: raw MASH stage-1 quantizer bits
mash_stage2_bits.mem: raw MASH stage-2 quantizer bits
mash_combined_*: digitally cancelled multilevel stream presented to the CIC
For zero-based n >= 2, combined[n] = stage1_bipolar[n] - (stage2_bipolar[n] - 2*stage2_bipolar[n-1] + stage2_bipolar[n-2])/mash_interstage_gain.
The first two combined samples are zero, matching the MATLAB model initialization.
fir_coefficients_*: tap 0 first, signed 24-bit Q1.23
cic_output_*: golden CIC output before the FIR
fir_output_*: golden decimated FIR accumulator before normalization
output_codes_20bit_*: golden rounded and saturated ADC output codes

Timing:
The CIC accepts mash_combined sample 1 immediately after reset and emits its first output for that sample.
The FIR accepts CIC output 1 immediately after reset and emits its first decimated output for that sample.
Initial transient samples are intentionally retained for cycle-accurate comparison.
